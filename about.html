Our Neighborhood Commitment

Rooted in Georgetown, Kentucky

PPR LIQUOR is a neighborhood liquor store in Georgetown, Kentucky, offering a selection of bourbon, whiskey, wine, beer and spirits. We aim to make shopping convenient with a welcoming atmosphere and helpful service. Stop by our store on Paris Pike to explore our selection.

Georgetown and Scott County hold a special place in Kentucky's historic whiskey heritage. Surrounded by world-renowned horse farms and legendary bourbon distilleries, we take pride in connecting local enthusiasts and visitors with both everyday favorites and hard-to-find releases.

When you walk through our doors, you’ll find clean, well-stocked aisles, clearly marked prices, and a knowledgeable staff ready to assist whether you're selecting a celebratory bottle of champagne, stocking up for game day, or searching for a single barrel bourbon gift.
