Fair Neighborhood Policy

How We Handle Rare & Allocated Bourbon Releases

Bottles like Blanton’s Single Barrel, Buffalo Trace, Eagle Rare, W.L. Weller, and E.H. Taylor are produced in limited quantities by Kentucky distilleries and allocated through regional distributors.

1. First-Come, In-Store

We put allocated bottles on our shelves as they arrive for our local neighborhood shoppers.

2. Bottle Limits

Strict one bottle per customer limits apply to rare allocations so more community members can enjoy them.

3. Call to Verify

Because shipments sell quickly, call us at (502) 370-4392 to ask about today's shipment status.
